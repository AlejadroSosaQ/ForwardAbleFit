🚲 Our Story

More than a trainer.
A bridge.

ForwardAble was born from lived experience. As a parent of a child with autism, our founder knows firsthand the gap that exists between clinical therapy and real-world independence.

We're not therapists — and we don't try to be. We are adaptive fitness coaches who specialize (through vast experience) in functional movement, coordination, and life skills like bike riding that open doors to confidence and freedom.

We work where families feel safest: at home, at a comfortable pace, with trust at the center of everything.

Why ForwardAble

Not clinical.
Not generic.

Most options fall into two buckets: expensive clinical therapy with limited hours, or standard personal training that isn't adapted. We fill the gap.

✓
Your voice, your feedback

We adapt to how each person communicates — verbal, non-verbal, or anything in between. As sessions progress, we create space for feedback in whatever form feels natural, so every client can tell us what's working and what isn't.

✓
Home-based & flexible

Training in a familiar environment reduces stress and increases engagement — especially for individuals with autism.

✓
Trust-centered approach

We build relationships first. Progress follows. Families in our community are our biggest advocates.

Personal experience
